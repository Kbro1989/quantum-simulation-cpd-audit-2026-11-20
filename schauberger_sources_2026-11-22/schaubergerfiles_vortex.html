The Vortex File

The Vortex File

Viktor Schauberger's public record here is forestry and water engineering, not free energy. PKS-hosted patent scans document torrent control (AT 113487, 10 June 1929), log-flume timber work, Wasserführung (AT 138296, 10 July 1934), and spring-like drinking water (AT 142032, 11 June 1935). Patent-office legal status is still pending. Implosion motors, the Repulsine as a flying machine, and living-water health claims remain PKS narrative or later folklore. This file is historical, not a construction manual or medical guide.

Historical archive, not a clinic

This is a historical document archive, not a clinic, workshop, or energy kit. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease. It does not instruct anyone to drink, spiral, magnetize, or “vitalize” water as therapy, and it does not publish construction or operating plans for dual-use apparatus (Repulsine, home power plant, drinking-water processes). Those objects appear only as historical or evaluation records. Vortex hydrodynamics and cavitation physics belong at waterimplosion.com. Seek licensed medical and engineering advice elsewhere.

FAQ

Direct answers for high-intent questions.

Did Viktor Schauberger invent free energy?

Not in the documents this archive can stand behind. PKS-hosted patents cover torrent control, log flumes, water guidance, a jet turbine, and a spring-like drinking-water process. Implosion-as-surplus-power remains PKS narrative or later folklore. Physics of real vortices and cavitation is discussed at waterimplosion.com, which does not treat perpetual motion as proven.

What is a Repulsine, according to this file?

A device name in the PKS biography timeline, reported as constructed in Vienna in 1940 with wartime sequels. This archive does not hold original drawings or independent institutional records. The Implosion & Repulsine page keeps that gap visible. This file is not a construction guide.

Are Schauberger’s patents real?

PKS hosts 31 patent-scan PDFs, and first-page headers for several Austrian numbers (including AT 113487, AT 138296, AT 142032, AT 117749) have been read here by eye. Official patent-office legal-status confirmation at DPMA, EPO, or the Austrian office is still pending. Google Patents is a secondary index only.

Is “living water” a medical treatment?

No claim in this file establishes that. AT 142032 is a historical process patent for spring-like drinking water. It is not clinical evidence. This archive does not advise anyone to drink, spiral, or vitalize water as therapy, or to delay licensed care.

Where are the primary documents?

Patent scans are listed from the PKS patents page. Contemporary press issues are ANNO full-issue PDFs. The public source manifest catalogs 121 records. Civil registry, employment files, and Repulsine drawings are named gaps on the Evidence page.

How should I cite this page?

Cite the underlying patent scan, ANNO issue, or PKS page first. If you cite the desk, use: “The Vortex File,” Schauberger Files, https://schaubergerfiles.com/vortex-file/, accessed 19 September 2026, and keep the evidence stamp attached.
